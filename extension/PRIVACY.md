# Privacy policy · WeryAI Image Compressor

Last updated: 2026-10-20

WeryAI Image Compressor compresses images entirely on your device. Optional AI editing uses WeryAI's separate website.

- **No uploads.** Images you add are compressed inside your browser and are never sent to WeryAI or any other server. The only exception is the opt-in "Edit photo with AI" item below.
- **No analytics.** The extension does not collect browsing history or analytics. The selected image is passed to WeryAI only through the opt-in AI editing action described below.
- **Local preference.** Your Auto / Light / Dark theme choice is saved in localStorage on this device. It is not sent to a server; selected images are not saved between page sessions.
- **Web images.** When you choose "Compress image" on a web page, the extension asks for permission to read images from that one site, then downloads that image directly from the site into your browser. You can revoke the permission at any time in `chrome://extensions`.
- **Edit photo with AI (opt-in).** Only when you choose "Edit photo with AI (WeryAI)" on an image, the extension opens WeryAI's AI photo editor on www.wery.ai and places that one image in the page's upload box, the same as if you had picked the file yourself. From then on WeryAI's own privacy policy applies. Compression never sends images anywhere.
- **Other links to WeryAI.** Other menu items and links marked "WeryAI ↗" only open www.wery.ai in a new tab; no image is passed.
